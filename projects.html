Research Paper

Researcher (data collection)

Overview

My team and I wrote the paper “Exploration of Student Perceptions and Experiences Toward Responsible AI in Digital Grief Support: Student Perspectives on Deathbot Interaction”. It explores how BINUS students perceive and experience deathbots, AI that reconstructs the personality of a deceased person from their digital traces, as psychological support while grieving. The study used a qualitative descriptive-phenomenological approach with 30 students who ran a Gemini-based deathbot simulation, analyzed with Braun and Clarke’s thematic analysis and the Kübler-Ross grief stages model.

What I did

  • Led the data collection phase of the study.
  • Ran structured interviews with respondents after the simulation.
  • Explored their emotional responses, level of dependence, and views on the ethical issues of using deathbots in grief.
